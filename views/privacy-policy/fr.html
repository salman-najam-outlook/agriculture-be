Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data by the Organization:

Dimitra (BVI) Incorporated processes your personal data to enhance your experience with our software applications and to provide personalized services. This processing is based on your consent and is necessary for the performance of a contract with Dimitra to which you are a party.

Legitimate Interests of the Organization:

Our legitimate interests include improving our services, preventing fraud, and ensuring network and information security. Where applicable, we may also process data for the legitimate interests of third parties, such as our business partners or governments.

Recipients of Individual’s Data:

Your data may be shared with:

  • Service providers and partners who assist with our business operations.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

Transfer of Individual’s Data to Third Countries:

Personal data may be transferred to countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses according to relevant EU Commission specifications.

Data Retention Period:

Your data is retained as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this notice, or until you withdraw your consent. The criteria for determining the retention period include the duration of your relationship with us and associated legal obligations.

Data Subject’s Rights:

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of your data, object to processing, and request data portability. You also have the right not to be subject to automated decision-making based on your personal data.

Right to Withdraw Consent:

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Right to Lodge a Complaint:

If you have concerns about our use of your personal data, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

Consequences of Failing to Provide Personal Data:

Provision of requested personal data is part of a contractual requirement with Dimitra. Failing to provide required personal data will result in loss of software functionality and degraded user experience.

Personal Profiling and Automated Decision-Making:

Personal data will not be used for the purposes of profiling users or making automated decisions based on personal data.
